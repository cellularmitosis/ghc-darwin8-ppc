## What this enables on your side (none required)

Nothing changes for the cross-build path you ship as v0.12.0 —
that already works. Where r8 matters is the **native-on-Tiger**
case: a Tiger PPC user who runs our shipped clang-8-tiger to
build C/C++ that interops with your stage2 GHC's RTS now gets
the matching ABI without needing the cross-clang. If your README
mentions "use the cross-clang for RTS-touching C code," r8 lifts
that workaround.
